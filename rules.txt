TYLER'S JOB SCREENING RULES

These are the rules the triage tool uses to decide Apply or Pass.

=== HARD NO (automatic Pass, no exceptions) ===

Pass immediately if ANY of these are true:

1. (MOVED - see LONG SHOT below. A degree or enrollment requirement on
   its own is no longer an automatic Pass.)
2. The job title contains Engineer, Scientist, Developer, or Researcher.
3. Requires Python, SQL, Java, C++, or any ML framework (TensorFlow,
   PyTorch) as a must-have.
4. Requires 3 or more years of experience.
5. The title contains Senior, Staff, Principal, Lead, Manager, Director,
   or Head of.
6. The job is basic data labeling with no real judgment, like tagging
   images, drawing boxes, or plain transcription.
   NOT a hard no: paid AI training, AI evaluation, or AI red teaming work,
   where I review, grade, or try to break AI model answers.
7. The role is sales quota carrying: SDR, BDR, Account Executive, or any
   commission-only pay.
8. The job is unpaid, equity-only, or an "exposure" opportunity.
9. The job is not in the Seattle area (Lynnwood, Edmonds, Shoreline,
   Seattle, Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Woodinville) and is not remote.

=== LONG SHOT (degree or enrollment required) ===

A degree requirement, a CS-major requirement, or "must be a currently
enrolled student" is NOT an automatic Pass on its own.

If the posting requires a degree or enrollment BUT nothing else on the
hard-no list is broken, mark it APPLY. Tyler wants to try these anyway.
Cap the fit score at 50 and put "long shot: degree or enrollment
required" in red_flags so he knows what he is walking into.

If the degree requirement comes WITH any other hard-no (engineer or
scientist title, required Python/SQL/ML, 3+ years, senior title, and so
on), it is still a Pass.

=== WHAT A GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE ===

Score higher when the posting has these:

- Titles like: AI Operations, AI Enablement, Operations Assistant,
  Operations Coordinator, Workflow Automation, Founder's Associate,
  Business Operations, Program Coordinator.
- Says entry level, 0 to 2 years, recent grad, or no experience required.
- Paid AI training, AI evaluation, or red teaming work (reviewing,
  grading, or stress-testing AI model answers).
- Mentions AI tools by name: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, or
  "generative AI."
- Mentions automation tools: Zapier, Make, Airtable, or no-code.
- Mentions documentation, SOPs, process mapping, or training others.
- Mentions spreadsheets, Google Sheets, or Excel as a main tool.
- Mentions customer communication, scheduling, or coordination.
- Company size is 1 to 200 people.
- Willing to train, or describes learning on the job.

=== WHAT I BRING (for judging fit) ===

- 8 Anthropic Academy courses (Claude, Claude API, MCP, agent skills)
- Zapier Building AI Agents and Building Intermediate Zaps
- Google AI Essentials
- 3 years part-time at a sign company, including scoping an AI intake tool
- Run my own Rover pet care business, 5.0 rating, all admin myself
- 4.0 GPA in college business coursework, no degree completed
- Beginner at Python. I can read and modify code, not build from scratch.

=== BORDERLINE RULES ===

- "Preferred" or "nice to have" requirements do NOT trigger a Pass. Only
  "required" or "must have" do.
- A degree listed as "or equivalent experience" is NOT a hard no.
- If the posting is vague about requirements, lean Apply and note it as
  a gap.
- Hybrid roles in the Seattle area count as local.

=== OUTPUT ===

For each posting, return:
- decision: Apply or Pass
- score: 0 to 100
- reasons: the top 3, one line each
- red_flags: which hard-no rules were triggered, if any
- lead_with: the 2 things from my background to put first in the
  application
